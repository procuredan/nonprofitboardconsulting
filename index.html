The Challenge

When great leaders work at cross-purposes

A nonprofit board filled with accomplished, well-intentioned leaders should be a tremendous asset. And it can be.

But when the roles between board and staff aren't clearly defined — when governance blurs into operations, or when board energy isn't pointed in the right direction — even the most talented people can work against each other without realizing it.

That's not a failure of character. It's a failure of structure. And structure can be fixed.

What I Do

Practical consulting grounded in real experience

I work with nonprofit executive directors and their boards to clarify roles, align expectations, and build the kind of working relationship that lets both sides do their best work.

My approach starts with assessment — understanding your organization, your board composition, and where the friction is coming from. From there, we build a practical path forward together.

Engagements are customized to your situation. Some organizations need a single facilitated session. Others benefit from ongoing partnership. We'll determine what's right for you.

I work with clients nationwide — in person and via video.
